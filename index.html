Welcome to Our Institution

A beacon of Islamic education in Ghana, nurturing minds and souls since establishment

Khalid Ibn Walid Institutes is dedicated to providing quality education that combines academic excellence with Islamic values. Located in Ghana, we offer comprehensive educational programmes from Kindergarten through Senior High School, alongside specialized Arabic language education, Islamic studies, and traditional Makaranta Quranic education.

Our institution is committed to developing well-rounded individuals who excel in their academic pursuits while maintaining strong moral character and deep understanding of Islamic principles.
