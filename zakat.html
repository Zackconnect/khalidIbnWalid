About Zakat

The third pillar of Islam - purifying wealth through giving

Zakat is a mandatory act of worship and one of the five pillars of Islam. It requires Muslims who meet certain criteria to donate a portion of their wealth to those in need. By giving your Zakat through Khalid Ibn Walid Institutes, you support Islamic education and empower the next generation of Muslims.

We ensure that your Zakat is distributed according to Islamic guidelines to eligible recipients including students in need, support for educational programmes, and community members facing hardship. Your Zakat helps provide scholarships, learning materials, and support for those striving to gain Islamic knowledge.

Zakat Eligibility & Distribution

Following Islamic guidelines for Zakat distribution

The Poor (Fuqara)

Students and families who lack sufficient means to meet their basic needs and educational expenses.

The Needy (Masakin)

Those facing financial difficulties that prevent them from accessing quality Islamic education.

Those in Debt (Gharimin)

Individuals burdened by legitimate debts who need assistance to regain financial stability.

In the Path of Allah (Fisabilillah)

Supporting Islamic education projects and programmes that benefit the community.

Wayfarers (Ibnus Sabil)

Travelers or those stranded who need assistance for their journey.

Scholarships

Full and partial scholarships for deserving students to pursue Islamic education.

Calculating Your Zakat

General guidelines for Zakat calculation

💰

Zakat al-Mal

2.5% of total wealth held for one lunar year, above the Nisab threshold.

🌾

Zakat al-Fitr

Fixed amount paid before Eid al-Fitr, equivalent to one Sa' of staple food.

⚖️

Nisab Threshold

Minimum amount of wealth required before Zakat becomes obligatory (equivalent to 87.48g gold or 612.36g silver).

For precise calculation, consult with a knowledgeable Islamic scholar or use a verified Zakat calculator.
