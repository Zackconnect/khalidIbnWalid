Primary School Programme

Building strong academic foundations with Islamic values

About Our Primary Programme

Six years of comprehensive education (Class 1-6)

Our Primary School programme follows the Ghana Education Service curriculum while integrating Islamic teachings throughout all subjects. Students from Class 1 through Class 6 receive a well-rounded education that prepares them for Junior High School and beyond.

We emphasize both academic excellence and character development, ensuring that our students not only achieve high standards in their studies but also grow in their understanding and practice of Islam.

Core Subjects

Following the GES curriculum with Islamic integration

English Language

Reading comprehension, writing, grammar, vocabulary, and literature studies following the national curriculum.

Mathematics

Numeracy, problem-solving, geometry, measurement, and mathematical reasoning skills development.

Integrated Science

Basic scientific concepts, nature study, and hands-on experiments to develop scientific thinking.

Social Studies

Ghanaian history, geography, civics, and social studies with Islamic perspectives where relevant.

Our Languages

Ghanaian language options (Twi, Ga, Hausa, etc.) to preserve cultural heritage and identity.

ICT

Basic computer skills, digital literacy, and introduction to technology for the modern world.

Islamic Education Component

Spiritual development alongside academic growth
