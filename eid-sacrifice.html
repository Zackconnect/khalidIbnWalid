About Layyah Services

Professional Eid al-Adha sacrifice arrangement

Layyah (also known as Udhiyah) is performed during Eid al-Adha in remembrance of Prophet Ibrahim's (peace be upon him) devotion and obedience to Allah. At Khalid Ibn Walid Institutes, we organize Layyah services to help the community observe this important religious practice conveniently.

We ensure that all animals are healthy, meet Islamic requirements, and are slaughtered according to Islamic guidelines. The meat is distributed among the donor, family, friends, and the needy, helping share the blessings of Layyah with our community.
