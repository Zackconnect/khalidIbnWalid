Admissions Information

Everything you need to know about applying to Khalid Ibn Walid Institutes

We welcome applications from students of all backgrounds who are committed to academic excellence and Islamic values. Our admission process is designed to be straightforward and transparent, ensuring that every family understands the requirements and expectations.

Admissions are open for all levels from Kindergarten through Senior High School. We encourage early applications as spaces are limited. Our admissions team is available to guide you through the process and answer any questions you may have.

Admission Requirements

Documents and information needed for application

KG Admission

  • Birth certificate
  • Immunization records
  • 2 passport-size photos
  • Parent/Guardian ID
  • Age: 4-6 years

Primary & JHS Admission

  • Birth certificate
  • Previous school report
  • Transfer certificate (if applicable)
  • 2 passport-size photos
  • Parent/Guardian ID

SHS Admission

  • BECE results slip
  • Birth certificate
  • JHS leaving certificate
  • 2 passport-size photos
  • Parent/Guardian ID

Application Process

Simple steps to secure admission

  1. Complete the Application Form: Fill out the admission form below with accurate information.
  2. Submit Required Documents: Provide all necessary documents as listed above.
  3. Pay Application Fee: Pay the non-refundable application fee of GHS 50.
  4. Assessment/Interview: Students may be required to take a placement test or attend an interview.
  5. Admission Decision: You will be notified of the admission decision within 5 working days.
  6. Acceptance & Enrollment: Complete enrollment by paying fees and attending orientation.
