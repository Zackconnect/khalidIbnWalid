Junior High School Programme

Preparing students for BECE success and future academic excellence

About Our JHS Programme

Three years of preparation for BECE (Form 1-3)

Our Junior High School programme prepares students for the Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE) while continuing their Islamic education journey. Over three years (Form 1-3), students build on their primary foundation and develop the knowledge and skills needed for Senior High School.

We maintain high academic standards while ensuring that our students grow in their Islamic practice and character development. Our track record in BECE examinations reflects our commitment to excellence.

BECE Core Subjects

Comprehensive preparation for national examination

English Language

Advanced reading comprehension, essay writing, grammar, and literature analysis for BECE success.

Mathematics

Algebra, geometry, statistics, and problem-solving skills aligned with BECE requirements.

Integrated Science

Physics, chemistry, biology, and agricultural science concepts for comprehensive science education.

Social Studies

Ghanaian government, economics, geography, and social issues with Islamic perspectives.

RME / Islamic Studies

Religious and Moral Education with focus on Islamic teachings and character development.

BDT / ICT

Basic Design and Technology or Information and Communication Technology skills.
