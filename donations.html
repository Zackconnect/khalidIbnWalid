About General Donations

Your support makes quality Islamic education accessible

At Khalid Ibn Walid Institutes, we believe that financial constraints should never be a barrier to accessing quality Islamic education. Your general donations and Sadaqah (voluntary charity) help us provide free and subsidized education to students who would otherwise be unable to afford it.

Every contribution, no matter the size, makes a meaningful impact. Your donations support teacher salaries, learning materials, facility maintenance, and scholarships for deserving students. By donating, you invest in the future of our community and earn continuous rewards (Sadaqah Jariyah).
