Our Academic Programmes

Programme Overview

A complete educational journey from KG to Senior High School

Khalid Ibn Walid Institutes offers a comprehensive educational pathway that follows the Ghana Education Service curriculum while integrating Islamic values and teachings throughout. Our programmes are designed to provide students with a strong foundation in both secular and religious education.

From the foundational years in Kindergarten through the critical transition years of Senior High School, we ensure that every student receives personalized attention, quality instruction, and the spiritual guidance needed to become well-rounded individuals.
