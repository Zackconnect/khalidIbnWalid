Makaranta & Quranic Education

Traditional Quranic memorization and Islamic sciences for spiritual excellence

About Our Makaranta Programme

Preserving the tradition of Quranic education

Makaranta represents the traditional system of Quranic and Islamic education that has been the cornerstone of Muslim communities for centuries. Our Makaranta programme combines traditional methods with modern educational approaches to provide authentic Quranic education.

Students engage in Quran memorization (Hifz), Tajweed (proper recitation), and the study of Islamic sciences under the guidance of qualified Quranic teachers. This programme runs alongside our regular academic curriculum, allowing students to excel in both secular and religious education.

Programme Components

Comprehensive Quranic and Islamic education

Quran Memorization (Hifz)

Systematic memorization of the Holy Quran with regular review (Muraja'ah) to ensure retention. Students progress at their own pace with individualized attention.

Tajweed & Recitation

Proper Quranic recitation rules including pronunciation, articulation points, and rhythmic patterns. Students learn to recite beautifully and correctly.

Islamic Sciences

Study of Fiqh, Hadith, Seerah, and Islamic theology alongside Quranic education for comprehensive Islamic knowledge.
