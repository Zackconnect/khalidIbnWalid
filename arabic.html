Arabic Language Education

Mastering the language of the Quran for deeper understanding and connection

About Our Arabic Programme

From alphabet to fluency - a comprehensive Arabic learning journey

Arabic is the language of the Quran, and understanding it opens doors to deeper spiritual connection with Islamic teachings. Our Arabic Language Education programme is designed to take students from complete beginners to proficient readers, writers, and speakers of Arabic.

Through systematic instruction, practice, and immersion, students develop the skills needed to understand Quranic Arabic, engage with Islamic texts, and communicate in Arabic. Our programme is integrated across all levels from KG to SHS.

Programme Levels

Progressive learning from basics to advanced

Beginner Level (KG - Primary)

Introduction to Arabic alphabet, letter recognition, basic reading, writing, and simple vocabulary. Focus on correct pronunciation and letter formation.

Intermediate Level (JHS)

Grammar fundamentals, sentence construction, expanded vocabulary, reading comprehension, and basic conversation skills. Introduction to Quranic Arabic.

Advanced Level (SHS)

Advanced grammar, literature analysis, complex reading comprehension, fluent conversation, and in-depth study of Quranic Arabic and classical texts.
