Senior High School Programme

Preparing students for tertiary education and future success with Islamic values

About Our SHS Programme

Four years of quality secondary education (SHS 1-4)

Our Senior High School is a private institution offering quality secondary education across multiple programme tracks. Students prepare for the West African Senior School Certificate Examination (WASSCE) while continuing their Islamic education in a supportive environment.

With experienced teachers, modern facilities, and a commitment to both academic and spiritual excellence, our SHS programme prepares students for admission to universities, polytechnics, and other tertiary institutions in Ghana and beyond.
