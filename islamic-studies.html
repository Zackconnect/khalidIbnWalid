About Our Islamic Studies Programme

Nurturing faith, knowledge, and Islamic character

Islamic Studies is at the heart of our educational philosophy. Our comprehensive programme covers Quran, Hadith, Fiqh (Islamic jurisprudence), Islamic history, and the Seerah (biography) of Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him). Students develop a deep understanding of Islam that guides their daily lives.

Through age-appropriate instruction, we ensure that students at every level from KG to SHS receive Islamic education that builds their faith, strengthens their character, and prepares them to be practicing Muslims in the modern world.

Core Islamic Subjects

Comprehensive coverage of Islamic sciences

Quran Studies

Memorization (Hifz), recitation with Tajweed, understanding of meanings, and Tafsir (exegesis) of selected surahs.

Hadith Studies

Study of authentic Hadith collections, understanding the sayings and actions of Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him).

Fiqh (Islamic Jurisprudence)

Practical Islamic rulings on purification, prayer, fasting, zakat, hajj, and daily life according to Islamic law.

Seerah (Prophet's Biography)

Study of the life of Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) as a role model and source of guidance.

Islamic History

Overview of Islamic civilization, contributions to world knowledge, and history of Muslim civilizations.

Aqidah (Islamic Beliefs)

Understanding core Islamic beliefs, the pillars of faith, and theological foundations of Islam.

Level-Specific Focus

Age-appropriate Islamic education at each stage

KG & Primary

Stories of prophets, basic Islamic manners, short surahs memorization, daily duas, introduction to prayer, and Islamic character building through stories and activities.

Junior High School

Deeper study of Fiqh, Hadith, Seerah, Islamic history, understanding of Islamic rulings for daily life, and preparation for practicing Islam independently.

Senior High School

Advanced Islamic studies, contemporary Islamic issues, comparative religion, Islamic ethics in modern contexts, and preparation for being informed Muslim adults.
