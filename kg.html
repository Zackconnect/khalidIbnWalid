Kindergarten Programme

Nurturing young minds with love, care, and Islamic values

About Our KG Programme

Building strong foundations for lifelong learning

Our Kindergarten programme provides a warm, nurturing environment where children aged 4-6 years begin their educational journey. We focus on developing foundational skills in literacy, numeracy, and social interaction while introducing Islamic values and teachings in age-appropriate ways.

Through play-based learning, structured activities, and guided exploration, our young learners develop confidence, curiosity, and a love for learning that will serve them throughout their academic career.

KG Levels

Two years of foundational education

KG 1

For children aged 4-5 years. Focus on basic recognition of letters, numbers, shapes, colors, and social skills. Introduction to Arabic alphabet and simple Islamic prayers.

KG 2

For children aged 5-6 years. Advanced preparation for Primary 1 with reading readiness, basic mathematics, writing skills, and continued Islamic education.

Curriculum Highlights

What your child will learn

📖

Language & Literacy

Letter recognition, phonics, vocabulary building, storytelling, and early reading skills.

🔢

Mathematics

Number recognition, counting, basic shapes, patterns, sorting, and simple problem-solving.

🎨

Creative Arts

Drawing, coloring, crafts, music, and creative expression to develop fine motor skills.

🤝

Social Development

Sharing, cooperation, empathy, and building positive relationships with peers and teachers.

ع

Arabic Basics

Introduction to Arabic alphabet, simple words, and basic Islamic phrases in Arabic.

🕌

Islamic Values

Stories of prophets, Islamic manners, daily duas, and introduction to prayer.

Typical Daily Schedule

A balanced day of learning and play

  • 7:30 AM - 8:00 AM: Arrival and Morning Assembly with Islamic prayers
  • 8:00 AM - 9:30 AM: Language and Literacy Activities
  • 9:30 AM - 10:00 AM: Snack Break and Play Time
  • 10:00 AM - 11:30 AM: Mathematics and Numeracy Activities
  • 11:30 AM - 12:00 PM: Arabic and Islamic Studies
  • 12:00 PM - 12:30 PM: Lunch Break
  • 12:30 PM - 1:30 PM: Creative Arts, Music, or Physical Activity
  • 1:30 PM - 2:00 PM: Review and Preparation for Home
